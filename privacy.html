How Digital Brand Lift handles information shared through our website and contact channels.

Last reviewed: November 2026

When you submit a project inquiry, we may receive your name, business name, email address, telephone number, selected service and project details. If you contact us through WhatsApp or email, those platforms process your messages under their own privacy policies.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to respond to requests, prepare quotations, communicate about projects, deliver agreed services and maintain necessary business records.

Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information. Website hosting, email, messaging, analytics and payment providers may process technical or contact information when you use their services. Their own terms and privacy policies apply.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to limit access to project information. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for communication, project delivery, business records or legal obligations.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about information you have submitted or request correction or deletion where legally appropriate.
