Frequently asked questions

Questions before getting started?

Here are some useful details about our services and packages.

Can I buy only one service?

Yes. You can choose website design, social media marketing or Google Business Profile setup separately. Combined packages are optional.

Are domain and hosting fees included?

Not automatically. Domain registration, paid hosting and other third-party costs are separate unless specifically agreed in writing.

Will my website work on phones?

Our website designs are prepared to adapt to mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can you guarantee Google Maps visibility?

No. Google controls verification, approval, ranking and visibility. We provide setup assistance and guidance but cannot guarantee Google's decisions.

How do I start?

Choose a service or package and send an enquiry through WhatsApp. We will discuss your requirements before work begins.

Do you accept custom requests?

Yes. Send us your requirements and we can discuss whether the requested work is something we can provide.
