Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before ordering.

Here are answers to common questions about our services, pricing and project process.

How do I place an order?

Choose a package or select individual services using our quotation builder. Submit your details through WhatsApp. We will review your requirements and confirm the project scope, final price and next steps before work begins.

Are the prices displayed on this website fixed?

The listed prices are fixed starting prices for the standard deliverables described. Projects that require additional concepts, extensive revisions, unusual formats or specialised creative work may require a separate quotation. Any additional cost will be discussed before the work proceeds.

Can I order just one service?

Yes. You do not have to purchase a complete branding package. You can select individual services such as a logo, flyer, poster, business card or social media graphic according to your needs.

Can I combine a package with additional services?

Yes. You can select a package and add other standard services to your quotation. The calculator will show the combined estimated amount. We will check for overlapping deliverables before confirming your order.

What if I need something that is not listed?

Send us a description of your project. Specialised branding, large campaigns, custom illustrations and other complex assignments are assessed individually. We will provide a quotation based on the agreed requirements.

How many revisions are included?

Each package specifies its included revision rounds. Standard individual services include one revision round unless otherwise agreed. A revision means reasonable adjustments to the approved design direction, not a completely new project brief. Additional work can be quoted separately.

How long does a design project take?

Delivery time depends on the complexity of the project, the number of deliverables and how quickly the required information and feedback are provided. We will agree on a realistic timeline before confirming the order.

Do your prices include printing?

No. The displayed prices cover design services only unless a written quotation specifically includes printing. Printing, physical materials and delivery are separate costs.

Which file formats will I receive?

The formats depend on the service and the agreed project scope. Common delivery formats may include PNG, JPG and PDF. Editable source files and specialised production formats should be discussed and agreed before the order is confirmed.

Do you work with clients outside Uganda?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from businesses and individuals in Uganda and other countries. International project requirements, currency and payment arrangements will be discussed before an order is accepted.

How do payments work?

Payment arrangements will be explained and agreed directly before work begins. Do not send money to an unverified personal number or account. Always confirm the official payment details and obtain written confirmation of your order.

Can I request an in-person discussion?

Yes. Contact us first through WhatsApp to discuss your project and arrange whether an in-person meeting is practical.
