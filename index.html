Frequently asked questions

Questions customers may have

Can I order one service without a package?

Yes. You can request an individual service or a custom combination.

How much do services cost?

Prices depend on the work required. Request a quotation and confirm the price before paying.

Do you serve customers outside Uganda?

International enquiries are welcome. Currency, payment and delivery arrangements must be agreed before accepting an international order.

How long does a project take?

The delivery date depends on the project scope and will be agreed in writing before work begins.

Can I request changes?

Yes. The included revisions will be stated in your quotation. Additional work outside the agreed scope may attract extra charges.

How do I pay?

Payment instructions will be provided after your quotation is accepted. Verify the recipient and amount before sending money.

Does submitting the form place an order?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp message. Your order is confirmed only after both sides agree on the details.

Service policies

Clear expectations for every project

Quotations & payment

Every project receives an agreed scope and quotation. Deposit requirements, payment arrangements and balances must be confirmed before work begins.

Revisions & delivery

Included revisions and delivery dates are agreed for each project. Extra work outside the agreed scope may cost more.

Privacy

Design ownership
