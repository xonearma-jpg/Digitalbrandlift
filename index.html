About Digital Brand Lift

Helping businesses present themselves with confidence.

Digital Brand Lift is a creative branding and design service focused on helping businesses communicate their value through clear, consistent and attractive visual materials.

Our purpose

Make professional-looking creative services more accessible to entrepreneurs, small businesses and growing organisations.

Our approach

Understand your business, agree on the deliverables, create the design and make agreed improvements.

Who we serve

Start-ups, shops, restaurants, fashion businesses, event organisers, professionals and organisations in Uganda and beyond.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions.

Can I order one service without buying a package?

Yes. You can request an individual service or ask for a custom combination.

How much do your services cost?

Prices depend on the work required. Request a quotation and confirm the price before paying.

Do you serve customers outside Uganda?

We welcome enquiries from Uganda and international customers. Payment method, currency and delivery arrangements must be agreed before an international order is accepted.

How long does a project take?

The delivery date depends on the project scope and will be agreed in writing before work begins.

Can I request changes?

Yes. The number and type of included revisions will be stated in your quotation. Extra changes may require an additional fee.

How do I pay?

Payment instructions are provided directly after your quotation is accepted. Always verify the recipient and agreed amount before sending money.

Does submitting this form place an order?

No. It opens WhatsApp with your enquiry. Your order is confirmed only after both sides agree on the details.

Working clearly

Service information and policies.

Quotations and payment

Every project receives an agreed scope and quotation. Payment arrangements, deposit requirements and any balance must be confirmed before work starts.

Revisions and delivery

Included revisions and delivery dates are agreed for each project. Additional work outside the agreed scope may attract extra charges.

Privacy

Information you submit through this form is passed to WhatsApp when you choose to send it. This website does not have a customer database or collect form submissions on a server.

Refunds and cancellations

Cancellation and refund arrangements must be agreed in writing before payment. Any applicable refund will depend on the work already completed and the agreement with the customer.

Customer responsibilities

Customers should provide accurate project details and have permission to use any logos, photographs, text or other materials they supply.

Design ownership

Ownership and permitted use of final approved work, editable files, fonts and third-party assets must be clarified in the project agreement.

These are starter service terms, not a substitute for legal advice. Confirm the final business policies before accepting paid orders.
