Frequently asked questions

Questions before getting started?

Here are answers to some common questions.

Do I need to buy a package?

No. You can request an individual service. Packages are optional combinations for customers who need more than one service.

Are the sample designs completed client projects?

No. They are demonstration concepts created to show possible layouts. Your actual project will be designed around your business requirements.

How do I make payment?

Payment arrangements are confirmed directly through WhatsApp. We use manual payment confirmation. Do not send money until you have received and verified the agreed payment details.

Are monthly plans automatically renewed?

No. Monthly plans are requested and confirmed manually. There are no automatic deductions from your mobile money or bank account.

Can you guarantee that my business will rank first on Google?

No. Google rankings depend on many factors. We can help with profile setup and good management practices, but cannot guarantee a particular ranking.

Are domain and hosting costs included?

Not automatically. Domain registration and hosting may involve separate third-party charges. These costs will be explained before you approve any work.

How do I get started?

Select a service or package, complete the enquiry form and send your request through WhatsApp.
