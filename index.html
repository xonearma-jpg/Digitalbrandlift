Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Find answers before starting your project.

Can I order one service without buying a package?

Yes. You can request an individual service or a custom combination that suits your needs.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the work required. Contact us for a quotation and confirm the price before making payment.

Do you serve customers outside Uganda?

Yes, international enquiries are welcome. Currency, payment arrangements and delivery details are agreed before accepting a project.

How long does a project take?

Delivery time depends on the size and complexity of the project. The agreed timeline will be included in your quotation.

Can I request changes to my design?

Yes. The number of included revisions will be agreed before work begins. Additional changes outside the agreed scope may attract extra charges.

How do I pay?

Payment instructions are provided after the quotation is accepted. Always verify the payment recipient and amount before sending money.

Does sending an enquiry confirm my order?

No. Your WhatsApp message is an enquiry. Your project is confirmed only after both sides agree on the scope, price and payment terms.

Which files will I receive?

File formats and whether editable source files are included will be agreed in your quotation.

Quotations and payment

Each project has an agreed scope and quotation. Payment amounts, deposit requirements and payment arrangements must be confirmed before work begins.

Revisions and delivery

Included revisions, delivery dates and final file formats are agreed for each project. Additional work outside the agreed scope may attract extra charges.

Privacy

Information entered in the enquiry form is placed into a WhatsApp message when you submit it. This website does not operate a customer database or store form submissions.

Cancellations and refunds

Cancellation and refund arrangements should be agreed in writing before payment, taking account of work already completed and any costs already incurred.

Customer responsibilities

Customers should provide accurate project information and have permission to use any images, text, logos or other materials they supply.

Ownership and usage rights

Ownership, permitted use, editable files, fonts and third-party assets should be clarified in the project agreement before delivery.
