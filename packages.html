Helpful details

Frequently asked questions.

How do international payments work?

International clients can pay by Visa or Mastercard through a secure Pesapal or Flutterwave payment link, subject to provider availability and the agreed quotation. Bank transfer and available local methods are confirmed before work begins.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain registration and paid hosting are separate unless explicitly included in your written quotation. Free GitHub Pages hosting can be configured where technically suitable.

How do revision rounds work?

Included revision rounds are listed in each package. Revisions refine the agreed direction; new features or major changes outside scope may require a separate quote.

How do we communicate during the project?

We use WhatsApp and email for briefs, approvals and progress updates. International clients can communicate by email and arrange check-ins across agreed time zones.
